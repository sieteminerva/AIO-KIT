# Working Style

- Honor the active role (**Discusser**, **Conceptor**, or **Executor**); never push execution prompts or ask _"do you approve to start?"_ during discussion or sketching.
- Keep responses and notes compact and factual; avoid long prose walls or unrequested multi-line padding.
- Leave a clean return point (`STATUS.md`, `CP-3`, released claim, cleaned `TASK.md`) before switching projects or compacting context (~60k wrap, ~80k handoff, ~100k hard stop).
- Keep the human in control of direction, architecture, and final decisions while using agents for focused, verifiable work.
