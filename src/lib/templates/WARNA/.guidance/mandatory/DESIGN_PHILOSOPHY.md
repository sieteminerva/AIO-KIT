# Design Philosophy

Connect visual craft, software engineering, physical production, and creative rhythm with clarity and intent.

> A product has its price, but its packaging adds value.
> We learn, we build, we innovate.

## Interface & Experience Standards

- Support both dark and light modes cleanly where UI applies.
- Keep visual and interaction structure clear, responsive, and mobile-first.
- Favor signal, restraint, and readability over visual noise.
